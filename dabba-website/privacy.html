Privacy policy

Dabba Calorie Tracker. Last updated 21 November 2026 . Data fiduciary: Shreyas P, VIT Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. paramshreyash@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

DataWhy we need it
Email address or Google account ID (none for guest accounts)To create your account and sign you in.
Age, sex, height, weight, waist, activity, sleep, stress, diet, region and climateTo calculate your calorie and nutrient targets.
Health conditions you choose to tell us (diabetes, PCOS, thyroid, blood pressure, pregnancy, breastfeeding, cycle dates)To adjust your targets safely. Optional.
Weight entries and goal weightTo forecast where your eating habits lead. The forecast runs on your phone.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing. We don't use your location, contacts or microphone.

Consent

We process your health and body data only with your explicit consent, given when you create your profile. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account; we then stop processing and erase your data.

Who processes your data for us

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and database), hosted in the [asia-south1 (Mumbai)] region.
  • Open Food Facts, which receives only a scanned barcode number to look up product nutrition.
  • If you use Dabba in device-only mode, nothing is sent to us at all: your data stays in your browser.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Database rules ensure each account can read and write only its own data, and limit what can be written to the leaderboard. If a personal data breach happens we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Profile → Download my data.
  • Guest accounts: guest data is tied to your browser. If you clear it before saving your account with Google or email, it can't be recovered.
  • Correction: edit your details in the app at any time.
  • Erasure: Profile → Delete account and all data, or see how to delete your account.
  • Grievances: email our grievance officer above. We respond within [7] days. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Retention

We keep your data while your account exists. We delete it when you delete your account, or after [24] months of inactivity after warning you by email or SMS.

Age

Dabba is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Not medical advice

Dabba gives general wellness estimates. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about medical decisions.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app before any material change to this policy takes effect.
